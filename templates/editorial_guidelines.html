EDITORIAL LIFECYCLE

The Publication Journey

Every magazine volume follows a standardized 5-stage editorial protocol to ensure literary excellence, rigorous scholarship, and seamless digital archiving.

STAGE 01 • INITIATION Academic Session Q1

Call for Manuscripts & Original Works

Departmental and hostel editorial boards issue an open invitation to students, faculty, and alumni. Submissions span creative essays, original Bengali and English poetry, scientific research digests, mathematical expositions, and campus fine arts.

Open Submissions Bilingual (EN / BN / SA)
STAGE 02 • SCRUTINY Mid-Session Review

Faculty Mentorship & Peer Editorial Review

Submitted works undergo rigorous scrutiny by departmental faculty advisors and senior student editors. Articles are validated for academic integrity, factual precision, cultural sensitivity, and ethical conformity with Swami Vivekananda's educational ideals.

Faculty Advisory Review Anti-Plagiarism Verification
STAGE 03 • PRODUCTION Pre-Press Typesetting

Micro-Frontend Web Layout & Typography

Student technical editors craft the magazine's digital micro-frontend. Using standard, semantic HTML5 and CSS3, sub-teams produce an autonomous standalone experience (index.html) that embeds natively into the Unified Portal reader with zero external telemetry or intrusive trackers.

Pure HTML5/CSS3 Responsive Typesetting
STAGE 04 • STANDARDIZATION Quality Assurance

Metadata Schema Validation & Packaging

Each publication creates its canonical meta.json descriptor adhering to the college schema specification. Editorial teams curate a high-definition 4:3 cover artwork (cover.jpg) and compile the directory into an authorized distribution ZIP.

meta.json Schema v1.0 4:3 Cover Aspect
STAGE 05 • ARCHIVAL Final Release

Two-Factor Authenticated Admin Ingestion

The Chief Administrator logs in using Two-Factor Authentication (Email OTP) and uploads the verified ZIP archive through the Administrator Portal. The platform sandboxes the archive, verifies path safety, updates the catalog index, and publishes the edition instantly across campus networks.

2FA OTP Verified Zero-Downtime Deployment
INSTITUTIONAL CODE

Editorial Standards & Academic Ethics

All publications bearing the seal of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira adhere strictly to foundational academic values.

Academic Integrity

Strict zero-tolerance policy towards plagiarism, unauthorized synthesis, or uncredited reproduction. All factual claims and scientific citations must be verifiable by departmental supervisors.

Linguistic Dignity

Respect for the literary heritage of Bengali, English, and Sanskrit. Authors are encouraged to embrace scholarly vocabulary, grammatical precision, and authentic regional linguistic nuance.

Character & Ideals

In alignment with Swami Vivekananda’s vision of man-making education, contributions should inspire noble character, scientific inquiry, philosophical reflection, and civic responsibility.

Open Scholarly Access

Vidyamandira magazines are freely accessible to students, researchers, alumni, and scholars worldwide without paywalls, tracking cookies, or commercial advertisements.
