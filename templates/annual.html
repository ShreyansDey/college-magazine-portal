The Vidyamandira Patrika represents the collegiate crown jewel of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira. Rooted in Swami Vivekananda's ideals of character-building and intellectual rigor, this annual volume weaves together student literature, philosophical inquiries, scientific essays, and campus retrospectives.
