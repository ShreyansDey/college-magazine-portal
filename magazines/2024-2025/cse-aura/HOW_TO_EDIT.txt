================================================================================
RAMAKRISHNA MISSION VIDYAMANDIRA — STUDENT MAGAZINE STARTER KIT
================================================================================
Designed so any student can create and publish a world-class magazine edition!
The layout, fonts, and responsive structure are already 100% pre-built for you.
You only need to insert your own articles, stories, poems, and photos.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN AND EDIT YOUR ARTICLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click "index.html" -> Click "Open with" -> Choose "Notepad" (or VS Code).
2. Press [Ctrl + F] to search for: ⭐⭐⭐
3. Follow the friendly step-by-step comments from beginning to end:
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 1: CHANGE YOUR MAGAZINE TITLE & SESSION] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 2: TOP EDITORIAL BAR] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 3: MAIN TYPOGRAPHIC MASTHEAD] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 4: HERO FEATURE STORY (LEAD ARTICLE)] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 5: CURATED ESSAYS & ARTICLES SPREAD] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 6: SEASONAL / ACADEMIC ISSUES & EVENTS] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 7: EDITORIAL COUNCIL & DESK MESSAGE] ⭐⭐⭐
      ⭐⭐⭐ [STEP 8: FOOTER CREDITS & RIGHTS] ⭐⭐⭐
4. Simply replace the dummy text with your student essays, poems, or stories.
5. Press [Ctrl + S] to save the file!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: ADDING YOUR PHOTOS (ZERO BROKEN IMAGES GUARANTEE!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you first open index.html in a web browser, all photo spots show neat,
stylish placeholder boxes with recommended photo sizes.

To replace any placeholder with your own real picture, simply save your image
into the "images" folder using the exact filename shown on that photo spot:

  * images/cover.jpg          -> Main Magazine Cover (800 x 500 px)
  * images/hero-feature.jpg   -> Top Lead Feature Story (900 x 600 px)
  * images/article-1.jpg      -> Essay 1 Photo (600 x 500 px)
  * images/article-2.jpg      -> Essay 2 Photo (600 x 500 px)
  * images/article-center.jpg -> Centerpiece Tall Portrait Photo (600 x 900 px)
  * images/article-3.jpg      -> Essay 3 Photo (600 x 500 px)
  * images/article-4.jpg      -> Essay 4 Photo (600 x 500 px)
  * images/editorial-desk.jpg -> Editorial Board / Group Photo (750 x 420 px)

Note: If you don't have a photo for an article yet, don't worry! The template
automatically displays a clean, branded box so your magazine never looks broken.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: HOW TO ZIP AND SUBMIT TO THE ADMIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Select all the files in this folder:
   - index.html
   - style.css
   - script.js
   - HOW_TO_EDIT.txt
   - images (folder)
2. Right-click on the selected files -> "Send to" -> "Compressed (zipped) folder"
   (On Windows 11: Right-click -> "Compress to ZIP file").
3. Name your zip file (for example: "vinay-bhavan-2025-26.zip").
4. Hand this zip file to your College Portal Administrator!
5. The admin will upload it through the Admin Portal, and your magazine will
   instantly be live for the entire college to read!
