Welcome to the official student publication for Session 2025–2026. This magazine is not merely an assemblage of printed sheets, but a living testament to the intellectual vitality, spiritual contemplation, and creative passion nurtured within our residential college.

Every essay, verse, and scientific monologue presented here has been curated by student editors, peer-reviewed for scholarly merit, and crafted in the immortal spirit of Swami Vivekananda: "Arise, awake, and stop not till the goal is reached."
