RAMAKRISHNA MISSION VIDYAMANDIRA — EDITORIAL SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
=====================================================================

1. FILE REQUIREMENTS:
   - Your entry point file MUST be named 'index.html'.
   - Place all your HTML, CSS, JS, and image assets in this directory.

2. RELATIVE PATHS (CRITICAL):
   - Always use relative links:
     * Correct: <link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">
     * Correct: <img src="images/photo.jpg">
     * Incorrect: <link rel="stylesheet" href="C:/Users/...">
     * Incorrect: <link rel="stylesheet" href="/style.css">

3. PACKAGING:
   - Select your files and compress them into a .zip file.
   - Hand the .zip file to the Portal Administrator for one-click upload!
